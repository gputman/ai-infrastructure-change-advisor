ChangePilot AI

AI-powered infrastructure change management for safer, faster enterprise deployments

ChangePilot AI is a product concept that combines infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and product workflow design to help enterprise teams evaluate infrastructure changes before deployment.

The product analyzes proposed changes, historical incidents, infrastructure dependencies, and operational risk signals to provide engineers and change approvers with a clear risk assessment, recommended mitigations, and deployment guidance.

Assessment Deliverables

This repository contains:

A product brief describing the opportunity, users, product vision, and release strategy
An interactive HTML click-through prototype
Day-one success metrics and product analytics recommendations
A sequenced GitHub issue backlog
Supporting product documentation
Product Opportunity

Enterprise infrastructure changes often depend on manual reviews, fragmented documentation, and institutional knowledge. This can lead to delayed approvals, overlooked dependencies, failed deployments, and avoidable recovery work.

ChangePilot AI is designed to help infrastructure engineers, platform teams, product owners, and change managers make more informed deployment decisions before changes reach production.

Core Capabilities
AI-assisted infrastructure change risk scoring
Historical incident and change-pattern analysis
Dependency and approval identification
Recommended deployment windows
Rollback-readiness guidance
Change review and approval workflows
Product analytics for change outcomes
Product Streams

This concept operates across three product streams:

Infrastructure: cloud, platform, deployment, observability, and change-management workflows
AI: risk analysis, recommendations, pattern recognition, and decision support
Product: discovery, workflow design, prioritization, release planning, and success measurement

Prototype

The HTML prototype will demonstrate the primary user journey:

Review the infrastructure change dashboard
Create or select a proposed change
View the AI-generated risk assessment
Review dependencies and recommended mitigations
Approve, revise, or defer the change
Review product and operational metrics
Project Status

This repository is being developed as a product engineering assessment. It contains no production backend, production service, or deployable infrastructure code.

The HTML prototype is the only code artifact and is intended solely to demonstrate product workflow, interaction design, and product judgment.
